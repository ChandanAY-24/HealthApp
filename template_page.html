Halsa

A personal health record app, in ink and lime

A complete personal health record app for one person and their care team, in an ink and lime look on white with a light and a dark theme, and it comes with sample data. Good for: a patient app, a clinic portal, a wellness or fitness tracker, or any app that shows health readings over time. How it works: each day's heart rate, blood oxygen, blood pressure, steps or sleep shows as a bar from the day's low to its high with a dot for the average; switch the measure or the range and the bars reshape, and tap a day to read it; ticking a dose off, logging a reading or a meal, and booking or moving an appointment all update the app straight away, most with Undo. Includes: an overview with four summary tiles, the vitals chart, a live pulse line, today's medicines, a breathing exercise, water, the next appointment, the latest lab results and the week's workouts; vitals pages for heart, oxygen, blood pressure and temperature with every reading; activity with daily rings, workouts and goals; sleep with each night's stages and recovery; nutrition with meals, water and iron; medicines with a day timeline, stock and refills, and four weeks of doses; records with lab results shown against their normal ranges, scans, visits and vaccines; care with appointments you book from free times, the care team and messages; reports with any measure over 30 or 90 days, a CSV download and a printable summary; settings with profile, devices, sharing, notifications, units, and a reset; and help. How to use: run it as it is to explore with the sample data, which is saved in your browser, then connect your own health data and clinic systems in place of the sample data, since there is no backend; Cmd+K searches everything, and it works on every screen from phone to desktop with a bottom tab bar on phones. Built with React, TypeScript and GSAP.
